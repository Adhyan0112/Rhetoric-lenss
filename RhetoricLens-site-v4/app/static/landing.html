05BUILT TO BE CAREFUL

A SIGNAL, NOT A SENTENCE

Precision over
easy accusations.

The classifier proposes a flag. Server-side checks decide whether it appears: confidence threshold, verified quote, short explanation and duplicate filtering. The score is calculated separately by the server.

RhetoricLens covers six fallacy types, and its configurable penalty weights are not scientifically validated. Detection can be imperfect. A flag is a cue to inspect the exchange—not a verdict about truth or who is right.
